Questions

What is AETHERA?

AETHERA turns your photo into a piece of art by giving it the look of a painting, sketch or any other image you choose.

How do I use it?

Upload the photo you want to change, upload an image with the look you like, pick how strong the effect should be, and press the button. Your artwork appears in a few moments.

What does style strength do?

It controls how much of the artistic look is applied. Low keeps your photo close to the original; high gives a bolder, more artistic result.

Which images work best?

Clear photos work well as your content. For the style, try paintings, sketches or anything with strong colors and textures.

Which file types can I upload?

JPG and PNG images work.

How long does it take?

Usually a few seconds to a minute, depending on the image size.

Can I download my artwork?

Yes. Press Download artwork under your result to save it to your device.

Is it free?

Yes, this demo is free to use.
